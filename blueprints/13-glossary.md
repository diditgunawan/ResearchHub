# 13 — Glossary
**PRISMA 2020** — reporting guideline for systematic reviews with a flow diagram of identification, screening, eligibility, inclusion.
**PICO/PEO/SPIDER** — frameworks for structuring review questions.
**Screening stage** — title/abstract, then full text.
**Conflict** — two reviewers' verdicts differ for an item/stage.
**Cohen's kappa** — chance-corrected agreement between two raters.
**Dedup** — identifying records that represent the same work.
**Work** — canonical scholarly record (article, preprint, etc.).
**Chunk** — retrievable text segment from a work or user file.
**RAG** — retrieval-augmented generation.
**Claim** — atomic statement in a synthesis with citations.
**Verification** — independent check that a cited chunk supports a claim.
**Grounded** — every claim backed by retrieved evidence.
**OA** — open access. **APC** — article processing charge.
**Quartile (Q1–Q4)** — journal ranking tier within a subject category (source-dependent).
**SINTA S1–S6** — Indonesian national journal accreditation ranks.
**ADR** — architecture decision record.
**Topic map** — clustering of a record set into labelled themes for discovery.
**Research family** — group of papers sharing a methodological/conceptual lineage within the analyzed set.
**Lineage** — parent–child links showing how approaches evolved, each backed by evidence.
**Evidence types** — citation, semantic similarity, keyword/concept overlap, chronology.
**Confidence band** — High/Medium/Low rating of a placement; Low ⇒ needs review.
**Positioning** — comparing a manuscript to the analyzed set to find its closest family and likely parents.
**Research gap (in-set)** — an unresolved issue visible within the analyzed papers; not a claim of global novelty.
**Extract-only mode** — upload mode that deletes the original file after extraction and keeps only derived data.
**Analyzed set** — the exact scope of records used in a map/lineage run.
**WSS@R (work saved over sampling)** — share of screening workload saved versus random order when a target recall R is reached.
**ATD (average time to discovery)** — mean position, as a fraction of all records, at which relevant records appear in a ranking.
**Prioritised screening (active learning)** — ranking unscreened records by estimated relevance; it advises order, it does not decide inclusion.
**PRISMA-S** — reporting extension for literature searches.
**SYNERGY** — open dataset of 26 labelled systematic-review screening sets.
**Search log** — record of each search's source, query, filters, date, and result counts.
